# Risiti — WhatsApp Daily Summary Template

## Purpose

This is the approved WhatsApp message template for a merchant’s daily business summary.

The in-app Daily Summary screen remains unchanged. This template applies only to the **Share on WhatsApp** action.

## Template

```text
*RISITI · DAILY SUMMARY*
{SHOP NAME}
{FULL DATE}
────────────────────

💰 *Paid sales: {TOTAL SALES}*
{PAID RECEIPT COUNT} paid receipt(s)

*PAYMENT BREAKDOWN*
M-Pesa: {M-PESA TOTAL}
Cash: {CASH TOTAL}

⏳ *Awaiting confirmation*
{PENDING TOTAL} · {PENDING COUNT} payment(s)
Not included in paid sales above.

🏆 *Top seller*
{TOP PRODUCT NAME} · {QUANTITY SOLD}

📦 *Restock soon*
• {PRODUCT NAME} — {STOCK REMAINING} left
• {PRODUCT NAME} — {STOCK REMAINING} left

────────────────────
_Recorded in Risiti_
```

## Rules

- Show paid sales and paid receipts only.
- Show M-Pesa and Cash totals separately.
- Show the pending-payment section only when at least one payment is awaiting confirmation.
- Show the top-seller section only when at least one product has been sold.
- Show the restock-soon section only when at least one tracked product is low or out of stock.
- Limit the restock list to the three most urgent products.
- Do not include profit, expenses, forecasts, or AI-generated advice in this version.

## Example

```text
*RISITI · DAILY SUMMARY*
Mama Njeri Shop
Sunday, 8 November 2026
────────────────────

💰 *Paid sales: KSh 12,450*
8 paid receipts

*PAYMENT BREAKDOWN*
M-Pesa: KSh 9,800
Cash: KSh 2,650

⏳ *Awaiting confirmation*
KSh 1,250 · 1 payment
Not included in paid sales above.

🏆 *Top seller*
Soda 500ml · 12 sold

📦 *Restock soon*
• Cooking oil — 3 left
• Sugar — 1 left

────────────────────
_Recorded in Risiti_
```
